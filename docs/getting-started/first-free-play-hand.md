# Your first Free Play hand

Free Play is the zero-risk way to try Stacked Poker: poker with play chips instead of USDC, a digital dollar that holds a stable value.

## Before you start

Nothing. Free Play needs no signup: you pick a name when you sit down. If you'd rather sign in, you can use a popular wallet (Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, Rainbow, WalletConnect) or let Stacked create one for you. See [Sign in](/docs/getting-started/connect-a-wallet).

You don't need USDC. Free Play has no real money involved.

## Steps

1. **Open Play.** You'll see a list of active tables, each tagged either Free Play or real-money. Look for a Free Play table at stakes you're comfortable with.
2. **Pick a seat.** A prompt asks how many chips you want to start with. Pick any amount: Free Play chips have no real value.
3. **Play.** Tables seat you instantly by default, Free Play included. The next hand deals you in. If the Host has turned off Auto-Accept, you'll see a pending status until they approve you.

## What's the same as real-money

The poker is identical: same shuffler, same rules, same interface, same action timer. Anything you learn in Free Play carries straight over to a real-money table.

## What's different

- **No buy-in.** No USDC moves, nothing to confirm, no network fee to pay.
- **Nothing onchain.** Free Play tables have no contract and no settlement. Chips aren't recorded onchain and are forgotten when you leave.
- **No earnings.** Free Play pays no one anything, because there's no real money in play.
- **No saved balance.** Each session starts fresh. There's no Free Play balance to carry between visits.

## When you're ready for real money

To try a real-money table, you'll need USDC in your wallet balance, on Base, an Ethereum-based network with very low fees. See [Add funds](/docs/getting-started/get-usdc-on-base), then [Your first real-money hand](/docs/getting-started/first-real-money-hand).
