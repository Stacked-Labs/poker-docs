# What's next

A lightweight view of what's on the way. No quarter commitments and no firm dates. When something ships, the relevant page is updated and this list shrinks.

## Recently shipped

- **Tournaments.** Buy-in tournaments alongside cash games, with prize pools paid out by the event's contract. See [Tournaments](/docs/playing/tournaments).
- **Buy USDC with a card.** Signed-in players can buy USDC, a digital dollar that holds a stable value, with a card inside Stacked Poker. Click **Add funds** in the header. See [Add funds](/docs/getting-started/get-usdc-on-base).
- **Run it twice.** On cash tables, when players are all-in before the river, everyone still in the hand can agree to deal the remaining cards twice. See [At the table](/docs/playing/at-the-table#run-it-twice).
- **Nicknames.** Choose the name other players see at the table and in Ranks. See [At the table](/docs/playing/at-the-table#your-name-at-the-table).
- **Free Tickets.** Hosts can give players a free seat in a tournament. See [Free Tickets](/docs/hosting/free-tickets).
- **Referrals.** Invite friends and get a share of the platform fee their games generate. See [Referrals and points](/docs/playing/referrals-and-points).

## On the roadmap

- **A shuffle you can check.** Each shuffle committed onchain before the hand, so anyone can confirm the cards weren't manipulated. Today's shuffler uses standard server-side cryptographic randomness.
- **External contract audit.** A formal third-party security review of the contracts. They're source-verified and unit-tested today. The audit would complete the trust story.
- **Expanded geographic availability.** We're working through what regions Stacked can operate in. See [Where Stacked is available](/docs/reference/availability).

## Ideas

Things that have been discussed but aren't committed:

- Voice and video chat at the table.
- More poker variants beyond Texas Hold'em.
- A public GitHub repo for the contracts.
