# What Hosts earn

Hosts earn 25% of the platform fee on every real-money hand played at a cash table they run, credited live as hands settle.

## How your share accumulates

Each real-money table is its own smart contract, a self-running program on Base, an Ethereum-based network with very low fees. Your USDC stays in that contract, not with Stacked Poker, and only the contract's rules can move it.

You earn **25% of the platform fee** on every real-money hand played at a cash table you host. It isn't a daily total or an end-of-session payout. It ticks up live as hands settle. After each hand at your table, the contract credits your share to a balance attached to that table, and the figure updates in real time at the table.

USDC is a digital dollar that holds a stable value.

At a tournament you host, the fee is part of each buy-in rather than taken per hand. Your share is held by the tournament contract and paid out once the tournament has finished and prizes are settled.

For how the platform fee itself is calculated, see [How fees work](/docs/your-money/fees).

## Where you see it

Inside each table you host, open the table's **Settings → Players** tab. You'll see your accumulated platform fee for that table, updated as hands settle, with the option to collect it.

This is per table: each table you host has its own balance and its own collect action. There's no "all earnings" view that adds up every table. If you host five tables, you have five balances to track and five places to collect from.

## Collecting your share

When you're ready to take your share from a table, open that table's Settings → Players tab and collect it. The contract sends your accumulated balance directly to your wallet, with no minimum amount, no schedule and no approval from anyone.

Two things worth knowing:

- **Network fees depend on how you signed in.** Gas is the small network fee for an onchain action. If you signed in with your own wallet, collecting is a transaction you sign, so you cover the gas for it on Base. If you signed in with a social account, email or phone, Stacked covers it. Gas while hands are settling is always on Stacked: you never pay to settle a hand.
- **The balance keeps growing while you wait.** Collecting doesn't pause anything. If hands are still settling at the table, your balance starts climbing again right after you collect.

You can collect at any point: every hand, every day, or once at the end of a long-running table.
