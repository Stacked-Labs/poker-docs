# Hosting a table

Anyone can host on Stacked Poker: a table for your group chat, or your own tournament. On the real-money tables you host, you earn 25% of the platform fee.

Hosts create tables, set the rules, and earn a share of the platform fee on real-money tables. There are no application gates and no fees to start. Sign in, open **Host**, and a new table contract is deployed for you on Base, an Ethereum-based network with very low fees. Stacked covers the deployment gas, the small network fee for an onchain action.

## Why host

If you run a regular game with friends, want a share of the platform fee on a public table, or just want to set the rules for how a game is played, hosting is the path. On real-money tables, you keep **25% of the platform fee**, and you collect it onchain whenever you like. ([How fees work →](/docs/your-money/fees)) Free Play tables don't generate earnings. They're a space for friends or a community to play without putting real money on the table.

You can also play at your own table. Hosting and playing aren't separate roles: you can take a seat at the table you created, or oversee from the side and let others play. Most Hosts who run friend games play, and most who run public tables don't.

## Creating a table

When you create a table, you set three things:

- **Stakes.** The small blind and big blind. The big blind has to be at least twice the small blind.
- **Mode.** Free Play (play chips, no earnings) or real-money (chips backed by USDC). USDC is a digital dollar that holds a stable value.
- **Visibility.** Public (it appears under Play) or invite-only (reachable only through your link).

That's it. You don't pick a buy-in range, because each player chooses their own buy-in. Stacked sponsors the gas to deploy the table contract, so creating a table is free for you.

## Seating players

Every new table starts with auto-seating on (Auto-Accept in your table's Settings tab). A player picks a seat, buys in and is dealt in. You don't have to do anything, so your table can fill while you're away.

If you'd rather choose who sits, as in a private home game, turn off Auto-Accept. From then on, each player who wants to sit shows up in a pending list at your table, and you get an in-app notification. The request shows who is asking and the buy-in they're proposing, for example "Alice requests a seat with a $200 buy-in." Approve and they're in. Decline and they aren't.

The choice is the same for public and invite-only tables. Each Host decides how their table is seated, open to all or hand-picked, and the platform doesn't set that rule for you.

## Running the table

Once the table is live, players play hands and Stacked's servers keep the game running. **You don't have to stay online for the table to keep running.** With Auto-Accept on, you have no required actions while the table is live. If you turned it off, approve new pending players when they arrive.

You can also step in between hands:

- **Kick a player.** You can remove a seated player. They get permission to take their stack back from the contract, and the money doesn't move automatically: they confirm on their side to get their USDC back.
- **Change stakes.** You can raise or lower the small or big blind.
- **Pause or end the table.** You can pause the table or close it entirely. When a table closes, every seated player gets permission to take their stack back. As with a kick, the money doesn't move on its own. Everyone confirms to actually get their USDC back.

Stacked can't send a player's money anywhere but to that player.

## Hosting Free Play tables

Free Play tables work the same way for the Host. Create the table, pick stakes, choose how players are seated, kick or pause as needed. There are just no earnings. Hosts run Free Play tables for the reasons people host home games: friends, communities, practice, fun.
