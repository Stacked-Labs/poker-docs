# Free Tickets

Free Tickets let you give players a free seat in your tournament, so a friend, a community member or a top finisher from your last event can play without paying the buy-in.

## What it is

A Free Ticket covers one player's first entry into one tournament. You decide how many free seats the tournament has, and you hand them out as links. Free Tickets work on both Free Play and real-money tournaments.

## How it works

- **Set your cap.** The number of free seats is the most Free Tickets that can be claimed in your tournament, however many links you share.
- **Share links.** On your tournament page, open the **Free Tickets** tab. Only you see it. A public link can be claimed by anyone, up to your cap, which suits a post in your community. You can also make links for one channel or one person: name each link, set how many times it can be used, or make single-use links to send to specific players. The tab shows how many seats each link filled, so you can see where your players came from.
- **Claiming a seat.** A player opens the link, completes your [entry tasks](/docs/hosting/entry-tasks) if you set any, and claims the seat. Each player can claim one Free Ticket per tournament.
- **Bring a friend.** Everyone in the tournament, whether they paid or came in on a Free Ticket, gets a few single-use codes (three by default) to invite friends. Those seats come out of the same cap, so invitations can never seat more players than you allowed. A friend who claims one becomes the inviter's referral — see [Referrals and points](/docs/playing/referrals-and-points).
- **Re-entries cost the buy-in.** A Free Ticket covers the first entry only. If a player busts and re-enters, they pay the normal buy-in.

## What a free seat changes

On a real-money tournament, the prize pool is built from paid buy-ins, so a free seat adds nothing to the pool and carries no platform fee. The free player competes for the pool like everyone else. If you set a guarantee, it still applies.

Claiming costs nothing for you or the player. Stacked covers the network fee to seat a free player on a real-money tournament.

## Walkthrough

Alice runs a free tournament for her community every Friday and a $5 buy-in tournament every Saturday. On Saturday's tournament she sets 10 free seats and sends a single-use link to each of Friday's top ten finishers.

Bob finished third on Friday. He opens his link on Saturday morning, joins Alice's Discord server because it's one of her entry tasks, and claims his seat. He's registered without paying anything.

Twenty other players pay the $5 buy-in. The prize pool comes from those twenty buy-ins, and Bob plays for it like everyone else. Bob busts in the first hour while late registration is still open and decides to re-enter. That second entry costs him the $5 buy-in.

Bob also got three bring-a-friend codes. He sends one to Carol, who claims a seat from Alice's remaining free seats and becomes Bob's referral.
