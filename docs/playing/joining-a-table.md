# Joining a table

Sitting down at a table on Stacked Poker takes a few taps: pick a seat, choose your buy-in, and you're dealt in.

## Finding a table

Open **Play** to see every public table currently running. Free Play and real-money tables show together, with a tag on each showing which mode. Each table shows its stakes, the Host, and how many seats are filled. Pick one and take a seat.

## Public and invite-only tables

Stacked has two kinds of tables:

- **Public** tables appear in Play and anyone can take a seat.
- **Invite-only** tables don't appear in Play. You reach them through a direct link the Host shares.

The flow once you're at the table is the same either way: pick a seat and buy in.

## Taking a seat

On a **real-money table**, taking a seat means buying in. Your buy-in goes into the table's contract, a program on the blockchain that holds the money under fixed rules, not to Stacked. You pick the amount and confirm. (See [Buying in](/docs/your-money/deposits) for the full step.) Once the buy-in confirms, you're seated and your chips show up in front of you.

On a **Free Play table**, there's no buy-in. You pick a seat, pick a chip amount, and you're in.

Tables seat you instantly by default. The next hand deals you in.

## When the Host approves players by hand

Every table is created by a player, and the Host decides how seating works. Auto-Accept is on for every new table. A Host who wants to choose who sits, as in a private home game, can turn it off in the table's Settings tab.

At those tables, you wait in a pending list until the Host approves or declines you. You'll see your pending status at the table.

**If the Host approves:** you're seated and your chips show up in front of you.

**If the Host declines or never responds:** on a real-money table, you can take your buy-in back to your wallet balance at any time. See [Cash out](/docs/your-money/withdrawals). On a Free Play table, there's nothing to take back. Just pick another table.
