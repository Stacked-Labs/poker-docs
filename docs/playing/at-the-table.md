# At the table

This page covers what you can do once you're seated: acting on a hand, sitting out, chat and leaving. The next hand deals you in.

## Acting on a hand

When the action is on you, you have the standard set of moves: fold, check, call, bet or raise. You see your hand, the community cards as they come, and the current bet to match.

Each decision has an **action timer**, a countdown that starts when it's your turn. If you don't act before it expires, the system folds you. The timer applies to every decision, including the showdown.

Your actions during a hand aren't recorded on the blockchain, in real-money or Free Play: nothing is confirmed by your wallet while a hand is in progress. The blockchain only sees the result once a real-money cash-table hand settles.

## Your name at the table

Seats and Ranks show your nickname. Set it from your profile: 3 to 16 characters, using letters, numbers, underscores, hyphens and periods. If you've also linked an X account, your nickname still shows by default, and you can choose to show your X handle instead. Until you pick a name, other players see a short code from your wallet. Pick a name so they see you. Playing Free Play as a guest, you pick a name when you sit down.

## Sit out and come back

If you need a break (bathroom, phone call, doorbell), tap **sit out**. You stay seated and your table stack stays at the table, but you're dealt out of upcoming hands. When you're ready, tap **I'm back** to rejoin the next deal.

Sitting out is the right move for short breaks. For longer ones, leave the table cleanly and rejoin later.

## Chat

Every table has a text chat among seated players. Use it for table talk or whatever culture the table runs on. Free Play and real-money tables both have chat.

## Between hands

Between hands, real-money cash tables briefly show a settlement indicator while the previous hand settles onchain. It's fast, typically under 5 seconds on Base, an Ethereum-based network with very low fees, so by the time the next deal starts, settlement is done. The indicator links to the transaction on Basescan, the public explorer for Base, if you want to check a specific hand.

Free Play tables don't have this: nothing settles onchain. Tournament hands don't settle one by one: a tournament pays out when it ends.

## Leaving the table

When you're done, stand up between hands (you can't leave mid-hand: finish the current one first), then tap **Cash out** to return your table stack to your wallet balance. If you signed in with your own wallet, you confirm the step. The full walkthrough is on [Cash out](/docs/your-money/withdrawals).

## Run it twice

On cash tables, when players are all-in before the river, Stacked Poker asks everyone still in the hand whether to run it twice. If every one of them agrees, the remaining cards are dealt twice and each board decides half of the pot. If anyone says no, or doesn't answer in time, the cards are dealt once as usual. Running it twice doesn't change what you win on average; it makes the result less swingy.

Run it twice is on by default at new cash tables, and the Host can turn it off in the table's settings. Tournaments always deal once.
