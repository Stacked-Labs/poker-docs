# Referrals and points

Invite friends to Stacked and earn a share of the platform fee their games generate, paid in USDC, a digital dollar that holds a stable value.

## What it is

Every player has an invite link. When someone joins through it, they become your referral. For 12 months after that, you earn 20% of Stacked's share of the platform fee (the part left after the Host's 25%) on every real-money game they play or host. Referrals also earn you points.

Your friend pays nothing extra. Your earnings come out of Stacked's share only: not out of the pot, the Host's share or anyone's stack.

## Your invite link

Open the [Leaderboard](https://stackedpoker.io/leaderboard) and find the **Invite friends** card. It has your link, and buttons to share it on X and Telegram. You can also set a custom referral code once: 3 to 20 letters, numbers, underscores or hyphens. It can't be changed after that.

A friend becomes your referral in one of three ways:

- They open your invite link.
- They enter your code in their own **Invite friends** card.
- They claim a bring-a-friend [Free Ticket](/docs/hosting/free-tickets) you shared.

A player only ever has one referrer, the first one, and nobody can refer themselves.

## When a referral counts

A referral starts as **invited**. It becomes **activated** when your friend takes their first real-money action: a cash-game hand or a paid tournament buy-in. Activated referrals count toward your points. Your earnings come from the platform fee, so they start with your friend's first real-money game.

## How earnings add up

- **Cash games.** The platform fee on each hand is shared evenly between the players dealt in. You earn 20% of Stacked's share of your friend's part.
- **Tournaments.** The platform fee is shared in proportion to buy-ins. You earn 20% of Stacked's share of your friend's part.
- **When your friend hosts.** You also earn 20% of Stacked's share of the platform fee on the tables and tournaments they host.

Free Play generates no earnings.

Earnings are ready to pay out 3 days after the games settle.

## Getting paid

Open your earnings from the **Invite friends** card. Once you have $10 or more ready on a network, request a payout. You get a payout code. Open a ticket in Stacked's Discord and paste the code, and a moderator sends your USDC. Payouts are sent by hand, so allow a little time.

## Points

Points are your standing on Stacked, not money. You earn them for real-money hands you're dealt into (more at higher stakes) and for completing quests. Referrals add to them:

- **Multiplier.** With 5 activated referrals, your points from hands are multiplied by 1.1. With 20, by 1.2.
- **Host bonus.** When a friend you referred first earns a real-money Host fee, you get 1,000 points, once per friend.
- **Most Referrals.** Activated referrals rank you on the Most Referrals board.

Quests are one-time tasks on the Leaderboard:

| Quest | Points |
|---|---|
| Follow Stacked on X | 500 |
| Join the Discord | 500 |
| Join Telegram | 200 |
| Create a table | 300 |
| Link your wallet (if you signed in with a social account, email or phone) | 300 |
| Play a paid tournament: finish one with a buy-in of $2 or more | 1,000 |
| Claim your NFT badge (also raises your multiplier by 0.2) | 3,000 |

## Walkthrough

Alice shares her invite link in her poker group's chat. Bob opens it and signs in, so he's Alice's referral, invited for now.

That weekend Bob buys into a $10 tournament. His referral is activated, and it counts toward Alice's points.

Over the next month Bob's tournaments and cash games generate $10 of platform fee for his part. Hosts get $2.50 of it, Stacked's share is $7.50, and Alice earns 20% of that: $1.50. Bob pays exactly what he would have paid anyway.

Bob then starts hosting a weekly tournament. Alice gets 1,000 points the first time Bob earns a Host fee, and from then on she also earns 20% of Stacked's share of the fees on Bob's tournaments, until 12 months after Bob joined through her link.

## FAQ

**Does my friend pay more because I referred them?**
No. Your share comes out of Stacked's share of the platform fee.

**My friend already joined through someone else's link. Can I still refer them?**
No. The first referrer is permanent.

**Do I earn anything from Free Play?**
No. Free Play has no platform fee, so there is nothing to share.
